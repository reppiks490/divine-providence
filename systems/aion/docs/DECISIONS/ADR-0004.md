# ADR-0004 - Freeze forecast evidence and label retrospective work

Date: 2026-09-23. Status: implemented research boundary.

## Decision

Prediction contract v2 records the event-ledger and gap-transition cutoffs
used by the forecast. `EventStore.frozen_frame(prediction_id)` reconstructs
that evidence frame even if a later historical import changes an ordinary
as-of query. A trusted local wall-clock issuance timestamp is stored; a past
horizon is labeled `retrospective_replay`. Neither a local-clock label nor a
free-text settlement source grants performance eligibility. Settlements are
explicitly `outcome_verified=false` until a reviewed independent outcome
adapter is built.

## Why

An immutable prediction row alone is insufficient. Without a ledger cutoff,
late imports can change the historical frame linked by its hash, and a
backdated decision can look like a prescient live forecast. Source timestamps
are caller supplied and must not be treated as provider attestation.

## Remaining gate

No return or accuracy figure may be promoted from these records. Forward
evidence needs an authenticated receipt clock and independently calculated
outcome observations. Legacy v1 predictions lack cutoffs and cannot be
reconstructed by `frozen_frame`.
