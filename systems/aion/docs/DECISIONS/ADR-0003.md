# ADR-0003 - PARALLAX starts with physical provenance, not signals

Date: 2026-09-23. Status: implemented inventory only.

## Decision

The first PARALLAX capability inventories each CSV member of an archive as a
physical source. It records archive/member hashes and ordinal, original header
positions including repeated names, and logical CSV row counts. Exact-byte
copies may share computation but never share physical identity. It does not
classify chart construction, infer release time, join instruments, compute
features, search analogs, open a protected holdout, or authorize execution.

## Why

Filename suffixes and parser-renamed duplicate columns cannot establish
representation identity. Quoted newlines also make a line count an unreliable
record count. Those errors would propagate into every later model or as-of
comparison. A local archive inventory is auditable without claiming real feed
evidence from a TradingView chart export.

## Acceptance and remaining work

Unit tests cover duplicate headers, quoted newlines, exact-byte copies,
sidecars, and malformed input. The output's verified/authorized flags are all
false. Owner/provider source manifests must establish instrument, contract,
chart mechanics, timezone, timestamp semantics, first-known availability and
rights before any decision-time replay or predictive claim. No sibling adapter
or trading path is connected by this ADR.
