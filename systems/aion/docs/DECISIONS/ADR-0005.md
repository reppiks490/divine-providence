# ADR-0005 — Distinguish derived-at-decision evidence from synthetic evidence

Date: 2026-10-01. Status: implemented research boundary.

## Decision

AION observation availability now has an explicit `derived_at_decision` basis for
locally derived NEXUS context that was computed for a specific decision instant but
may be persisted later.

This basis is deliberately different from `synthetic`.

A `derived_at_decision` observation must:

- be a `context` observation;
- have `event_ns == available_ns`;
- belong to a source whose `origin` is exactly `nexus_derived`;
- still satisfy `available_ns <= ingested_ns`; and
- retain its derivation/source-health lineage in the payload and source manifest.

It does **not** add a `synthetic` quality flag and it does not grant execution or
production authority.

## Why

NEXUS derivation and source-health records are real deterministic products of inputs
available at a decision instant. Labeling them `synthetic` was semantically wrong
and collided with AION's synthetic-source firewall. Labeling them as ordinary
`observed_receipt` would also be wrong when durable persistence happens after the
decision instant.

The new basis preserves the distinction between:

1. when the derived state was valid/available to the decision process;
2. when that state was later persisted into AION; and
3. genuinely synthetic scenario or fixture evidence.

## Anti-backdating boundary

The basis is intentionally narrow. A caller cannot use it for bars, trades, book
events, macro releases, or arbitrary historical sources. AION rejects mismatched
kind, clock, or source origin. Provider observations still require their own reviewed
receipt/release/bar-close semantics.

Any broader producer must receive a separately reviewed source contract rather than
reusing `derived_at_decision`.

## Compatibility

Historical v0.3 and v1.15 NEXUS contract identities remain immutable. Because this
changes the live AION sibling contract, the consolidated system requires a new
versioned sibling-contract snapshot and a new current PROMETHEUS pin after exact
Python 3.11 snapshot generation and cross-system validation.
