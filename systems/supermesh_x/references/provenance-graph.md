# Provenance Graph

Every material claim should be traceable through evidence nodes to source nodes. Preserve parent/derivation relationships so summaries, calculations, normalized records, and fused claims can be traced back to the underlying source family.

Do not double-count mirrors, syndicated stories, copied posts, or search results that ultimately derive from the same original material. Store canonical source, content hash, publication/event/availability/retrieval timestamps, provider, source class, and transformation parents.

A provenance graph is evidence plumbing, not proof of causation. Conflicting independent source families remain separate until resolved or explicitly reported as disagreement.
