# Crypto and on-chain mesh

Combine market and chain evidence without conflating them.

## Market plane

CoinGecko and other market feeds can provide price, market cap, liquidity proxies, categories, trends, gainers/losers, and news.

## On-chain plane

Blockscout can provide supported-chain address data, transactions, token transfers, holdings, NFTs, contract ABI/code, and read-only contract calls. Chain identity and block number/timestamp must be explicit.

## Visualization plane

ExoScope can provide interactive crypto market/chart views when available.

## Cross-checks

- token symbol collisions
- wrapped/bridged asset identity
- contract address and chain ID
- decimals/unit scaling
- block finality and reorg sensitivity
- centralized exchange price versus DEX/on-chain values
- wallet labels as claims unless verified

## Evidence fusion

Example: token research may combine market liquidity/trend, contract behavior, holder/transfer activity, protocol docs, governance/forum material, audits, and current news. Keep observation, inference, and speculation separate.
