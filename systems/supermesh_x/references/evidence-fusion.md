# Evidence fusion

## Evidence families

Two pages are not independent evidence if both repeat the same wire story, filing, press release, paper, or dataset. Group evidence into families by origin.

## Claim ledger

For each material claim track:

- claim text or normalized value
- supporting evidence IDs
- contradicting evidence IDs
- source-family count
- most authoritative source
- newest relevant source
- event time and retrieval time
- confidence and unresolved caveats

## Conflict resolution

Do not average incompatible facts. Diagnose disagreement by:

- time/version differences
- methodology differences
- geography/sample differences
- symbol/entity mismatch
- adjusted vs unadjusted values
- preliminary vs revised data
- copied secondary reporting
- genuine expert/scientific disagreement

## Confidence

Confidence is a structured judgment from authority, independence, freshness, completeness, consistency, and reproducibility. Never present a numeric confidence score as an objective probability unless it was calibrated for that domain.
