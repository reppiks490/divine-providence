A registered integration is not equivalent to a connected integration. Runtime state must be one of ready, degraded, quota-constrained, preflight-required, auth-required, unavailable or unverified. Only ready/connected providers may execute. Missing providers remain deferred and may be replaced by another provider implementing the same capability.
