# Substack intelligence integration

Substack is treated as a long-form research and narrative source.

## Acquisition hierarchy

1. Public publication RSS (`https://<publication>.substack.com/feed`).
2. Public post/archive pages through normal search/fetch/crawl.
3. User-authorized copies delivered through an authorized email/data connector when available.
4. Do not bypass subscriber-only access controls.

## Normalization

Publication, author, title, canonical URL, publication time, retrieval time, summary/body where authorized, mentioned entities/assets, claims, cited sources, topics, and source provenance.

## Uses

- Long-form macro, market, technology, geopolitical, and sector research.
- Discovery of expert commentary and source links.
- Narrative/claim tracking over time.
- Cross-checking thesis claims against primary sources and structured market data.
- Feeding market-mover event analysis when a public post itself becomes a widely circulated catalyst.

Substack commentary is evidence/context, not a privileged truth source. Weight it by author/source quality and corroboration.
