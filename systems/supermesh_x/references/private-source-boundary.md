# Private Source Boundary

SuperMesh-X separates public evidence from user-authorized private evidence. Private email, financial accounts, holdings, transactions, attachments, and other authorized private records may enrich local reasoning, evidence graphs, portfolio overlays, and user-requested summaries. They are not raw material for external/public search calls.

Default rule: **private -> public egress is denied**. A derived feature may cross the boundary only after an explicit transformation that removes raw content, account identifiers, addresses, attachment contents, and other private fields. Keep separate provenance lists for public and private evidence so citations, confidence, and audit trails never imply that a private source is public.

Research authority never becomes write authority. Email writes, financial-memory edits, account-linking actions, watchlist changes, resyncs, brokerage orders, deployments, and publishing remain separately gated.
