# Unified market intelligence workflow

1. Resolve named market movers and current officeholders/executives at runtime.
2. Pull public statements/social activity through direct public sources, search/crawl, news, video/transcripts, and authorized connectors.
3. Pull scheduled macro context through Forex Factory plus primary statistical/central-bank sources.
4. Pull market/fundamental/factor/crypto/on-chain context from the registered provider mesh.
5. If IBKR is authorized, enrich with portfolio/positions/account-aware market context and subscribed market data; do not place orders unless separately and explicitly instructed.
6. Pull relevant public Substack feeds/posts for long-form narrative and source discovery.
7. Deduplicate into Event Fabric clusters, preserve first-seen time, and build cross-asset shock graphs.
8. Check confounders, revisions, delayed-vs-live data, and competing events.
9. Emit evidence, confidence, counterfactuals, and uncertainty rather than unsupported causal claims.
