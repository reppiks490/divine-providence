## Strongest evidence-backed conclusions

1. The system uses multiple distinct structure families rather than one generic support/resistance object.
2. Major order blocks / icebergs are broad price zones with tier hierarchy.
3. Minor liquidity levels refine or sit around larger zones.
4. Fortresses / Mini Icebergs are a separate family.
5. Nano-Blocks / Price Rips are a separate family with a visible strength-color concept.
6. Same-side clustering is repeatedly highlighted in successful examples.
7. Price is often allowed to penetrate a zone before reacting.
8. Buy and sell structures are used symmetrically.
9. Zone-to-zone rotation appears repeatedly.
10. Same-side structure migration can accompany trend continuation.
11. VWAP, POC, Value High/Low, NYSE Open, Close, previous-day levels, POWERLINE and KEY LEVEL repeatedly overlap highlighted structures.
12. Named “Monsta” configurations appear to be special cases layered on top of the base taxonomy.
