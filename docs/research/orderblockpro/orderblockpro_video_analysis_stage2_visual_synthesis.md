# OrderBlockPro Tutorial Video Analysis — Stage 2 Visual Synthesis

## Scope
This synthesis is based only on visible chart/legend behavior from the 11 unique uploaded videos. It is **not** a substitute for the spoken tutorial rules. Anything dependent on narration is marked as pending transcript verification.

## Recurring architecture visible across the tutorials

### 1. Four visible structural classes
The OrderBlockPro legend repeatedly shows four classes:

1. **Major Order Blocks / Icebergs**
   - Super-Blocks
   - Mega-Blocks
   - Maxi-Blocks
   - Medi-Blocks
   - Mini-Blocks
   - Buy-volume family: green shades
   - Sell-volume family: red shades

2. **Minor Liquidity Levels**
   - Mega-Levels
   - Maxi-Levels
   - Medi-Levels
   - Mini-Levels
   - Buy-volume family: blue/cyan shades
   - Sell-volume family: purple/pink shades

3. **Nano-Blocks (Price Rips)**
   - Buy-volume family: green-to-light-green
   - Sell-volume family: red-to-light-pink
   - The legend explicitly includes a color-strength scale.

4. **Fortresses (Mini Icebergs)**
   - Buy volume: blue
   - Sell volume: yellow

### 2. Hierarchy and confluence are visually central
Across the videos, the chart frequently contains several horizontal structures concentrated in a narrow price band. Yellow hand-drawn circles repeatedly emphasize these dense clusters. The visual lesson therefore appears to distinguish isolated levels from **stacked/confluent zones**.

**Transcript verification still needed:** whether the narration assigns a formal priority score, minimum count of overlapping levels, or exact trade rule to these stacks.

### 3. Buy-side and sell-side structures are asymmetric but complementary
The tutorials consistently place:
- green/blue/cyan structures on buy-volume/support-side areas,
- red/purple/pink structures on sell-volume/resistance-side areas,
- yellow Fortresses as a separate highlighted sell-volume mini-iceberg class.

Price examples repeatedly move between those opposing families.

### 4. Repeated reaction patterns visible in examples
The following chart behaviors recur across multiple videos:

- approach into an upper red/magenta stack followed by rejection or rotation lower,
- approach into lower green/cyan stacks followed by stabilization or rebound,
- compression/ranging between upper sell-side and lower buy-side structures,
- sharp directional expansion after price leaves a dense cluster,
- multi-stage movement from one liquidity/block region to the next.

These are **observed visual patterns**, not yet authoritative strategy rules.

### 5. Instrument portability
The examples cover at least:
- **NQ Futures / Nasdaq 100 E-mini**
- **ES Futures / S&P 500 E-mini**

Tutorial 9 Update A includes both NQ and ES examples. This suggests the material is meant to generalize beyond one instrument, but the transcript is needed to determine whether parameters or interpretation differ by market.

### 6. Unresolved visual elements
Several white curved/stepped lines or envelopes appear across examples but are not identified in the visible legend crop. Their meaning should not be guessed. The narration must be used to determine whether they represent volatility bands, averages, session references, or another proprietary layer.
