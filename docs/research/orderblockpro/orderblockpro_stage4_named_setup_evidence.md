## Additional patterns strengthened by this batch

### A. Contextual-level confluence appears repeatedly important
The newest screenshots place highlighted block structures directly around:
- **VWAP**
- **POWERLINE**
- **POC**
- **NYSE OPEN**
- **CLOSE**
- **VALUE HIGH**
- **VALUE LOW**
- **PREV DAY HIGH**

This further supports the hypothesis that the heatmap objects are intended to be interpreted relative to broader market reference levels.

### B. Stacked same-side structures are repeatedly highlighted before large moves
Examples include:
- multiple green structures under price before an upside move,
- blue/green Fortress + block combinations near lows,
- multiple red structures above price before a downside rotation.

This is stronger evidence that **stack density / confluence** matters.

### C. Price can oscillate between opposing structural zones
The 7 Sep screenshot explicitly marks multiple upper red sell-side zones and lower green buy-side zones with arrows connecting the rotations.

This is one of the strongest visual examples yet for a **two-sided zone-to-zone framework**:
- lower buy-side cluster → upper sell-side cluster,
- upper sell-side cluster → lower buy-side cluster,
- repeat until one side is decisively accepted/broken.

### D. Continuation can occur through stair-stepped buy-side structures
The ES 2 Sep screenshot shows a persistent upward move with several blue buy-side structures appearing progressively higher in price.

Working hypothesis:
- in a directional regime, fresh same-side structures may act as **continuation / pullback support**, rather than all setups being reversal-only.

This is important because it broadens the preliminary framework from:
- “buy lower / sell upper”
to:
- **reversal + continuation**, depending on how new same-side structures migrate with price.

### E. Fortress and block categories are behaviorally distinct
The direct annotations “MONSTA-MINI FORTRESSES” and “MONSTA-MINI BLOCKS” strongly imply that OrderBlockPro treats these as separate structure families rather than interchangeable labels.

That distinction should remain explicit in any reconstruction or future code model.

## Stronger working model after Stage 4

A more complete visual reconstruction is now:

1. Identify the major buy-side and sell-side structural regions.
2. Classify the structure family: block, liquidity level, nano-block, or Fortress.
3. Detect **stacking / clustering** of same-side structures.
4. Check whether the cluster forms a named high-grade pattern such as:
   - Mini-Monsta Triple Blocks
   - Monsta-Mini Fortresses
   - Monsta-Mini Blocks
5. Check contextual overlap:
   - VWAP
   - POWERLINE
   - POC
   - NYSE OPEN
   - CLOSE
   - VALUE HIGH / LOW
   - PREV DAY HIGH / LOW
6. Observe how price enters the zone:
   - rejection,
   - absorption,
   - penetration,
   - reclaim,
   - consolidation,
   - or continuation.
7. Determine whether the market is:
   - rotating between opposing zones, or
   - stair-stepping with same-side structures in a directional trend.
8. Use the next opposing structural cluster as a candidate destination/decision area.

Again, steps 6–8 are a visual reconstruction and still require transcript verification before being treated as the official OrderBlockPro rule set.
