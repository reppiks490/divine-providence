- Preserve subsystem ownership boundaries and exact version identities.
- Prefer exact package/hash/test evidence over conversational claims.

- Do not silently merge, rename, overwrite, duplicate, or transfer authority among sibling systems.
- For any item in `MISSING_OR_UNPERSISTED_ARTIFACTS.md`, search/recover the real artifact before modifying or recreating it.
