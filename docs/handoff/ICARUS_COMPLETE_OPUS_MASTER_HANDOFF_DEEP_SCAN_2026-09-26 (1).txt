3. MASTER LOOP GOVERNOR and orchestration
3.1 Fixed/rotating topology
At the recovered governor design point, the Governor and SuperMesh-X occupied fixed slots while exactly three additional workers rotated.
Rotation criteria: remaining executable work, cross-loop leverage, dependency criticality, stagnation/age, ownership collisions, blocked state, and READY_TO_COMMIT proximity.
Workers marked BLOCKED, STALE, redundant, or READY_TO_COMMIT become swap candidates; replacements resume from state capsules rather than restarting.
Recovered active snapshots included: Governor + SuperMesh-X + VECTOR + Infrastructure + JANUS; later Governor + SuperMesh-X + AEGIS + Infrastructure + JANUS. ASCENSION, PROMETHEUS, Advanced CSV/NEXUS were paused in those snapshots.
Central Orchestration is distinct from MASTER LOOP GOVERNOR. Central Orchestration reconciles handoffs and global visibility; Governor owns worker allocation.
3.2 Proof-carrying handoff chain
Specialist output → Proof-Carrying Handoff → Durable Storage → Freshness Check → Central Orchestration → Consumer adoption
Infrastructure owns durable persistence/recovery of handoff envelopes.
ASCENSION owns evaluation/evidence semantics, not orchestration routing.
NEXUS owns corpus authority; consumers must not independently mutate it.
VECTOR/Icarus-style producers attach results to envelopes but do not self-authorize adoption.
Freshness/revalidation metadata is mandatory because historical verification can become stale.
3.3 Readiness vocabulary
Item | Recovered detail
BUILT | Implementation exists and local tests/compilation may pass.
VERIFIED | BUILT plus independent or integration verification for the declared scope.
READY_TO_COMMIT | VERIFIED plus artifact integrity, documentation, reproducible package state, and repository/release readiness.
RUN_OBSERVED | A loop or scheduler invocation was observed, but output was not directly verified.
PAUSED | Durable state intentionally preserved; work not currently active.
BLOCKED | A required external dependency/evidence/authorization is missing; system must fail closed.
